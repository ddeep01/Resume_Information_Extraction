SELECTED SOFTWARE AND TRANSFER
Developed web-based demo software1 for object and person retrieval from entire feature
length movies based on (Sivic and Zisserman, 2003) and (Sivic et al., 2005); about 7,000
lines of code.
Since 2008, I have been consulting on several projects involving large-scale visual search

the follow-up research, mobile visual search company Plink Art was the first UK start-up
bought by Google.
Software and demo for non-uniform deblurring from a single image, results of work of my
first Phd student O. Whyte are available online2.

PUBLICATIONS
I have published 40 papers in international journals and major peer-reviewed conferences. The
leading conferences in computer vision (ICCV, ECCV, CVPR), machine learning (NIPS) and
computer graphics (SIGGRAPH / TOG) have a low acceptance rate (typically below 25%), and
publications in their proceedings are considered more important than journal publications. The top
journals of the field are the International Journal of Computer Vision (IJCV) and the IEEE
Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (PAMI). Overall, my publications have
over 10,261 citations and my h-number is 33 (both obtained from Google Scholar). My
publications include:
